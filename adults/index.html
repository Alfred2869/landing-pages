Straight answers

Questions people actually ask

Including the honest one about who this is not for.

What does it cost?

Your costs are set out in writing in your service agreement after the free consult, before you commit to anything. No surprise report invoices.

In-home or in-clinic - which is better?

Whichever you will actually stick with. Home sessions build skills where you use them every day; the clinic suits people who focus better away from home. We offer both, and the therapist will tell you honestly which fits you at the consult.

What happens at the free consult?

Half an hour - a chat about your goals, a look at how you move and manage day-to-day, then an honest read and a written picture of what sessions would look like. No charge, no obligation.

Which NDIS funding does this use?

Self-managed, plan-managed and NDIA (agency) managed plans are all accepted. Have your plan handy at the free consult and the therapist will tell you honestly what it covers.

Do I need my own gym membership or equipment?

No. The therapist works with whatever you have - a home setup, a park, our clinic, or your local gym if you already train there. If equipment would genuinely help, that goes in the written picture before you spend anything.

Which areas do you cover?

Melbourne and Geelong, including Werribee, Point Cook, Hoppers Crossing, Tarneit, Craigieburn, Cranbourne, Clyde, Melton, Sunbury, Ringwood and surrounds. If you are outside these areas, ask us at the consult - we may still be able to help.

How soon can sessions start?

Typically within five business days of the free consult. Once you accept the service agreement, your first regular session is booked at a time that suits you.

Who is this NOT for?

Adults without NDIS therapy funding and people outside Melbourne and Geelong. This page is our adult service - for children's therapy, see our children's page. If any of these describe you, we will tell you honestly rather than waste your time.
